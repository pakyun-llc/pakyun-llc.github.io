Muteki Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026 | 日本語

"Muteki" (the "App") is an iOS application provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Our Principle — Your Motion Data Never Leaves Your Device

  • Motion data (step count, walking/running detection): The App uses motion data only to play BGM while you are walking and to display your steps and "invincible time". Motion data is never transmitted or shared with third parties. All processing happens on your iPhone.
  • Records (steps, invincible time, best record): Stored only on your iPhone and never sent to the Developer.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores your motion data or personal information.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following information to serve ads:

InformationPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if tracking is allowed)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address / approximate locationRegional ad delivery
Device information (model, OS version, etc.)Ad optimization and fraud prevention
  • You can decline or change tracking permission at any time via the iOS dialog (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). All features of the App remain available if you decline.
  • Ads are never shown while you are walking or while the BGM is playing.
  • For how Google handles data, see Google's policies and terms.

3. Device Features Used by the App

FeatureUsage
Motion & Fitness (CoreMotion)Step counting and walking/running detection, used solely to control BGM playback and record invincible time. Nothing is transmitted.
Background audioKeeps the BGM playing while you walk, even with the screen off.
