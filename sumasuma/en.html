Me on My Phone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Me on My Phone (the "App") is a camera app that places a nostalgic mobile phone or smartphone into your space so you can take composite photos and videos. This policy explains how the App handles information.

The App does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information.

2. What the App accesses, and why

The App accesses the following on your device. All processing happens on the device; nothing is sent to any server.

AccessedPurposeStored
CameraPreview and capture of composite photos and videosOn device only
MicrophoneAudio for video recording (only if you choose it)On device only
Photo libraryChoosing the image or video shown on the handset screen, and saving what you captureOn device only

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), device information, and approximate location for ad delivery and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data for details.

iOS: Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. All features remain available if you decline. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android: In the European Economic Area and other covered regions, a consent form is shown on first launch and ads are served according to your choice. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime in Settings > Privacy > Ads. All features remain available either way.

No analytics or other third-party services are used beyond the advertising SDK above.

4. Third Parties

Except for the advertising SDK described above, the App does not share your information with any third party. Photos and videos you capture are shared only when you choose to share them through your device's standard share sheet.

5. In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases such as removing ads. Payment is handled by the App Store on iOS and by Google Play on Android. The App never receives your card or payment details.

6. Storage and Deletion

Photos and videos you capture are saved to your device's photo library, and you can delete them yourself at any time. Uninstalling the App removes everything it stored internally.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. No sign-in or account is required, and no personal information is collected from any user, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change as features are added. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
