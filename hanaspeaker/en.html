Nose Speaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 | 日本語

Nose Speaker ("the App") is an iOS app provided by Pakyun LLC ("the Developer"). This policy explains how user information is handled.

1. Core principle — the Developer never receives your data

  • There is no server operated by the Developer. Neither your recordings nor anything captured by the camera or microphone is ever sent to the Developer.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

2. Camera and microphone

  • Camera: used to show your face on screen and to record video. Face detection (measuring how far your mouth is open) happens entirely on your iPhone; face data is never stored or transmitted.
  • Microphone: used to record the sound that is playing. While recording, surrounding sound is included in your video.
  • Recordings are stored only on your iPhone. You choose whether and where to share them.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following for ad delivery:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if tracking is allowed)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address / approximate locationRegion-appropriate ad delivery
Device information (model, OS version, etc.)Ad rendering and fraud prevention
  • Nothing captured by the camera or microphone is ever used for ad delivery or targeting.
  • You can decline or change tracking at any time (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). All features remain available if you decline.
  • See Google's policies for details.

4. Using the App safely

Do not push earphones into your nose. Holding them in front of your nose is enough for the App to work.

5. Deleting your data

All data lives on your device. Deleting the App removes everything it stored.
