Pants Puncher Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 | 日本語

"Pants Puncher" (the "App") is an Android application and a web application provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Our Principle — Camera Video Never Leaves Your Device

  • Front camera: Used only to show your own face as the background of the play screen. The video is displayed on screen in real time and is never sent to the Developer or any third-party server. Every game feature works without granting camera access.
  • Result card: Only when you tap "Share", the App renders the current screen (including the camera image) into a single image, stores it in the App's private storage on your device, and hands it to your device's share sheet. You choose whether and where to send it. The Developer never receives the image.
  • Scores: Your high score is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Developer.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores your video or personal information.
