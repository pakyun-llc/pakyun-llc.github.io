"Pyutto" Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

"Pyutto" (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information the Developer Collects

The developer does not collect or transmit any personal information.

  • Camera: Used only to display AR targets in your surroundings and to recognize your finger-gun hand pose. All hand recognition runs on your device (Vision framework); no video or recognition data ever leaves your device.
  • Game data such as high scores is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and approximate location for ad delivery and measurement. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How AdMob uses data.

Tracking for personalized ads occurs only if you allow it in the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. All features remain available if you decline. You can change tracking settings anytime under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Sharing with Third Parties

Except for the processing by the advertising SDK described above, the App does not share your information with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
