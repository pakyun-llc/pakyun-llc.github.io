"Imetan" Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

"Imetan" (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

The App is a vocabulary app that automatically generates an image for each word you add, so you can memorize words visually.

Information the Developer Collects

The developer does not collect any personal information. Study cards and generated images are stored only on your device. See below regarding transmission of words.

External API

To generate an image that matches a word you enter, the App may send that word to an external image-generation API. Only the entered word is sent; no personal information such as your name or contact details is ever transmitted. Generated images and study cards are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and approximate location for ad delivery and measurement. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How AdMob uses data.

Tracking for personalized ads occurs only if you allow it in the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. All features remain available if you decline. You can change tracking settings anytime under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases such as removing ads. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store, and the developer never receives payment information such as your credit card number.

Sharing with Third Parties

Except for the processing by the advertising SDK and the image-generation API described above, the App does not share your information with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
