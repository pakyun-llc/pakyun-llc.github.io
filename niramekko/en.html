Niramekko Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026 | 日本語

"Niramekko" (the "App") is an iOS app provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Our Principle — Face Data Stays on Your Device

  • Camera and face data: Smile judging uses the front (TrueDepth) camera and Apple's ARKit face tracking. Camera images, face geometry, and expression data are processed in real time only on your iPhone and are never stored or transmitted anywhere.
  • Result images: Shareable result images are generated on-device and leave your device only when you choose to share or save them.
  • Records: Your solo-mode best record is stored only on your device.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores your camera images, face data, or personal information.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following information to serve ads:

InformationPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if tracking is allowed)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address / approximate locationRegion-appropriate ad delivery
Device information (model, OS version, etc.)Ad display optimization and fraud prevention
  • Camera images and face data are never used for advertising.
  • You can decline or change tracking permission at any time in the iOS dialog (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). All features of the App remain available if you decline.
  • For how Google handles data, see Google's policies and terms.
