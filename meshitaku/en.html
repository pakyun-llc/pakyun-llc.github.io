Meshitaku Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

"Meshitaku" (the "App") is an iOS app provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Basic Policy — Your Data Stays on Your Device

  • Camera feed: Used only for 3D scanning of dishes (creating your "meshitaku" keepsakes). Images captured during scanning and the generated 3D models are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Scan data: 3D models, thumbnail photos, dish names, restaurant names, and notes are stored only on your device and are deleted when you remove the App.
  • Sharing: 3D models and photos are shared only when you explicitly use the iOS share feature.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores your personal information.

2. Advertising
