Petari Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

"Petari" (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information collected by the developer

The developer does not collect or transmit any personal information.

  • Camera: Used only to display your stickers in the real world through AR (augmented reality). All processing happens on your device; nothing is sent to external servers.
  • Photo Library: Accessed only to let you pick source photos for stickers and to save your captured photos. Subject cutout is processed entirely on your device (Vision framework).
  • Your stickers: Stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and coarse location for ad delivery and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in AdMob for details.

Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it in the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. All features work even if you decline. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Sharing with third parties

Except for the processing by the advertising SDK described above, the App does not share your information with any third party.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
