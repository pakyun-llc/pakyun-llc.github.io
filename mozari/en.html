Mozari Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Mozari is a photo app that pixelates only the areas you trace with your finger. Mozari ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information We Collect

The developer does not collect or transmit any personal information.

  • Photos & Camera: Used only to select photos (photo library) or capture a photo (camera) to pixelate. All processing happens on your device; photos are never sent to any server. Edited images are saved to your photo library only when you choose to save them.
  • Settings: Stored on your device only.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (IDFA), device information, and approximate location for ad delivery and measurement. See the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data for details.

Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it in the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. All features remain available if you decline. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Third Parties

Except for the ad SDK processing described above, the App does not share your information with any third party.

Changes

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
