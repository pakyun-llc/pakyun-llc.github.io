waowao Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 | 日本語

"waowao" (the "App") is provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy explains how user information is handled in the App.

1. Basic policy — the Developer does not receive your data

  • The Developer operates no servers. The camera picture, the information read from your face, and the videos you record are never sent to the Developer.
  • There is no account registration or login.

2. Camera

  • The front camera is used to show your face on screen, bend the picture as your face moves, and play sounds.
  • In the road mirror of "broken mirror", the back camera is used at the same time to show the scenery around the mirror. Nothing about faces or people is read from the back camera picture.
  • All processing of the picture happens on your device. The picture is never sent off the device.
  • The microphone is not used. The only sound in a recorded video is sound the App synthesizes on the device.

3. Face data (TrueDepth camera / face tracking)

  • What is collected: From the device's face tracking (ARKit face tracking on iOS, which uses the TrueDepth camera on some models), the App reads the approximate positions of facial features (eyes, nose, mouth, chin, forehead) and numbers describing your expression (mouth open, eyebrows raised, eyes wide, lips puckered, smile, one-eye blink, mouth left/right, tongue out, head tilt, how close the face is).
  • How it is used: Only to bend the picture of your own face shown on screen and to play matching sounds. It is not used to identify you, for face authentication, to build a face template, for advertising, or for analytics.
  • Storage and sharing: These positions and numbers are used in memory to draw the current frame. They are not stored, not sent off the device, and not shared with any third party. They are not passed to the advertising SDK.
  • Retention: They are overwritten with every new camera frame and nothing remains after the App is closed.

4. Recorded videos

  • The App records only when you start a recording or start "3 panels". A red mark is shown while recording.
  • A recorded video (the bent picture and the synthesized sound) is first kept in a temporary location on the device. If you choose "save" it is saved to your photo library; if you choose "share" it is handed to the destination you pick. If you leave the screen without doing either, the temporary file is deleted.
  • Photo library access is add-only. The App never reads your existing photos or videos.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect the following information to deliver ads.

  • The camera picture and face data are never used for ad delivery or targeting.
  • You can deny or change tracking permission at any time (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). The App works fully even if you deny it.
  • For how Google handles data, see Google's policies.

6. Deleting data

All of the App's data is on your device. Deleting the App deletes its saved settings. Videos saved to your photo library can be deleted from the Photos app.
