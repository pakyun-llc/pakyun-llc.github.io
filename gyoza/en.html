Drill Diner Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 | 日本語 | Support

"Drill Diner" (Japanese name: ドリル飯店; formerly Dumplings & Drill; the "App") is a game provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. The Developer does not receive your data

  • The Developer runs no servers. Your play history, scores and settings are never sent to the Developer.
  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • All game content (pictures, sounds, fonts) is inside the App, and playing uses no network. Only the ads below use the network.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Only your settings (drilling angle, music, sound, vibration, language) are stored on the device. They are never sent off it.
  • To decide when to ask for a rating, the Android version records on the device how many times you cleared a round and when you first did.

3. Vibration and sound

  • The App uses the device's vibration to convey the feel of the drill. You can turn it off under "Vibration" in Settings.
  • All sounds are generated inside the App. The microphone, camera, location, contacts and photos are not used.

4. Sharing

When you tap "Share" on the result screen, a line about your result and a store link are handed to the app you choose. What gets sent is up to you in that app.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads (a banner at the bottom of the title and result screens, and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds; never while you play). AdMob may collect the following for ad delivery.

  • In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA), a consent form appears first. You can change it any time from "Ad settings" on the title screen.
  • On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings. On iOS you can change tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. All features work without it.
  • See Google's policies for how Google handles data.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Deleting data

All App data stays on your device. Deleting the App removes its settings and records.
