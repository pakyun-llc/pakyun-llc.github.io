Karocho Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Karocho ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information the developer collects

The developer does not operate any server that collects personal information, and collects none.

  • Profile (height, weight, age, sex, activity level) and meal/exercise logs: used only to calculate your daily calorie balance, and stored only on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Camera & photos: used only to estimate the calories of your meals (see "AI calorie estimation" below).
  • Location: used only on-device with Apple's MapKit search to suggest nearby restaurants. It is never sent to the developer. All other features work without location access.

AI calorie estimation

When you use photo-based calorie estimation, the meal photo you take or select is sent to Anthropic's API (Claude) solely to estimate its calories. Photos are sent only when you run an estimation, and only to obtain the result. This feature is active only if you enter your own API key in Settings, and the key is stored only on your device. For how Anthropic handles data, see the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

Calorie estimates are approximate and must not be used for medical decisions.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data for details.

Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it in the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. The app remains fully functional if you decline. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Sharing with third parties

Apart from the AI calorie estimation (Anthropic) and the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) described above, the app never shares your information with third parties.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Important changes will be announced on this page.
