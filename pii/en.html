Pii Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026 | 日本語

"Pii" (the "App") is an iOS app provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Our Principle — Your Data Stays on Your Device

  • Videos and audio: The audio of videos you select is processed on your iPhone to detect NG words. The bleeped video is also generated on-device and saved to your photo library.
  • Speech recognition: Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition. When on-device recognition is available, nothing is sent over the network (depending on your device and OS, audio may be processed on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy).
  • NG word list: Words you add are stored only on your device and are never sent to the Developer.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores your videos, audio, or personal information.

2. Advertising
