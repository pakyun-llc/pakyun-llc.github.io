Fuwari Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Fuwari (the "App") is a camera app that floats a photo of your choice in midair over the camera view, letting you capture composite photos and videos. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

The App itself does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information. However, the App displays ads through a third-party advertising service (Google AdMob), which may collect certain information as described in Section 3.

2. Information processed on your device

The App accesses the following data solely for the features below. All processing happens on your device, and the App never sends this data to any external server.

DataPurposeStorage
Camera feedPreview and capture of composite photos and videosOn device only
Microphone audioRecording ambient sound in videos (only when you choose to)On device only
Photo librarySelecting photos to float, and saving your creationsOn device only

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads via Google AdMob so that it can be offered for free. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you tap "Allow" in the tracking permission dialog
  • Device information such as IP address, device model, and OS version
  • Usage data such as ad impressions and taps

If you do not allow tracking, non-personalized ads are shown and every feature of the App remains available. For details on how Google handles this information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Sharing with third parties

Except for the ad-related collection described in Section 3, the App does not share any user information with third parties. Photos and videos you capture are shared only when you do so yourself through the standard iOS share sheet.

5. Data storage and deletion

Captured photos and videos are saved to your device's photo library, and you can delete them anytime in the Photos app. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored inside the App.

6. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. No login or registration is required, and the App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features are added. Important changes will be announced on this page.
