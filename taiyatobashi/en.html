Tire Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 | 日本語

"Tire Launcher" (タイヤ飛ばし, the "App") is an Android application and a web application provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Our Principle — The Developer Collects Nothing

  • There is no server that receives your information. The App never asks for your name, email address or contact details.
  • Records: your best distance, win streak and number of clears are stored only on your device. Deleting the App deletes them.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.

2. Advertising

The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following in order to serve them.

InformationPurpose
Advertising IDAd personalisation, measurement, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate locationRegion-appropriate ads
Device information, app activity, crash logsAd delivery, fraud prevention, analytics
  • Users in the EU, UK and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form on first launch.
  • You can change or reset ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Device Features Used

FeatureUse
Internet accessFetching ads only
VibrationFeedback when the tire comes loose and when it lands

The Android version does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

4. The Web Version

The web version (pakyun.net/taiyatobashi) has no ads. It offers two extra features, both of which stay inside your browser.

  • Face mode: your front camera is shown behind the game. The video is only displayed on screen and is never sent to the Developer or any third party. The game is fully playable without granting camera access.
  • Recording mode: the screen and sound are recorded into a single video inside your browser. The video is handed to your device's share sheet only when you press "save", and you choose where it goes. If you do not press it, the video goes nowhere.

Records such as your best distance are kept in your browser's local storage.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children. The Developer does not knowingly collect information from users under 13.
