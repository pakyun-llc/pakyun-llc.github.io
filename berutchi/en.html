Berutchi Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Pakyun LLC ("we") explains here how user information is handled in the iOS app "Berutchi" (the "App").

1. We run no servers

The App uses only Apple iCloud (CloudKit) to carry messages. Your messages are stored solely in your and your friend's iCloud (private and shared databases). We operate no servers or databases of our own. We cannot see, collect, or store your messages, your pairs, or your usage.

  • Messages (up to 12 kana characters) live in a shared zone of the sender's iCloud private database, accessible only to the invited pair
  • Pair nicknames and message history for display are stored locally on your device only
  • Unpairing (blocking) deletes the corresponding shared zone
  • Data in iCloud is also governed by Apple's Privacy Policy

2. No account required

No account registration, email address, or phone number is required. Pairs are formed only by exchanging an invite link you generate; you cannot connect with anyone else.

3. Notifications

The App uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and local notifications to announce new messages. Notification permission is optional and can be changed anytime in iOS Settings.

4. Advertising

The App shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version, etc.)
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • Coarse location
  • Ad impression and tap data

On first launch, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is shown. All features work even if you decline (you will see non-personalized ads). See Google's Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data.

5. Purchases

The App may offer a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is processed by Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment details.

6. Abuse

The App is invite-only by design — you can only message a mutually invited pair, never strangers. If you receive abusive messages, use "Unpair (Block)" in the app to stop them, and please report via our support page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via the support page.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as needed. Material changes will be announced on this page.
