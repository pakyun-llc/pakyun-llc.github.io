Setsumeisho Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026 | 日本語

"Setsumeisho" (the "App") is an iOS application provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Core Principle — Your Manuals Never Leave Your Device

  • Scanned manual images and text: Page images captured with the camera or imported from Photos, and the text extracted from them, are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.
  • Text recognition (OCR): Performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework.
  • AI answers: On supported devices, the App uses Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models). Your questions and manual contents are never sent to the Developer or any external server. On unsupported devices, the App falls back to on-device full-text search.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores users' images, text, or personal information.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following information to serve ads:

InformationPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if tracking is allowed)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address / approximate locationRegion-based ad delivery
Device information (model, OS version, etc.)Ad optimization and fraud prevention
  • You can decline or change tracking permission at any time via the iOS dialog (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). All app features remain available if you decline.
  • The contents of your scanned manuals are never used for advertising.
  • For how Google handles data, see Google's policies and terms.

3. Device Features Used by the App

4. About AI Answers

AI answers are generated on your device based on the manual's contents, but they may not always be accurate. Always check the original manual for important operations and safety-related matters.
