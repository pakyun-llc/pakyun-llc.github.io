Manpoke Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 | 日本語

Manpoke ("the App") is an iOS app provided by Pakyun LLC ("the Developer"). This policy explains how user information is handled.

1. Core principle — the Developer never receives your data

  • There is no server operated by the Developer. Your steps, distance, starting point, goals and records are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the Developer.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

2. Location

The App uses your location to set the starting point of your journey.

  • It is read once, when you set your starting point. The App does not track you continuously or in the background.
  • The starting point is stored only on your iPhone and is never sent to the Developer.
  • If you do not grant location access, you can still use every feature by choosing a place by name with "Start from a city".

About data sent to Apple (important)

  • The App uses Apple's MapKit and CoreLocation to draw maps, resolve place names and search for places. By the nature of these features, the map area shown, the coordinates being looked up, and your search terms are sent to Apple.
  • This communication is required for the App to work as a map app. The Developer does not receive any of it.
  • See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this data.

3. Motion data (steps)

  • The App uses Motion & Fitness to count your steps and walking distance.
  • Steps and distance are stored only on your iPhone and are never transmitted or shared with third parties.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following for ad delivery:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if tracking is allowed)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address / approximate locationRegion-appropriate ad delivery
Device information (model, OS version, etc.)Ad rendering and fraud prevention
  • The steps, distance and starting point collected by the App are never used for ad delivery or targeting.
  • You can decline or change tracking at any time (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). All features remain available if you decline.
  • See Google's policies for details.

5. Shared images

You can create and share an image showing how far you have walked. The image is created on your iPhone, and you choose whether and where to share it. The Developer never receives it. The image does not mark your exact starting point.

6. Deleting your data

All data lives on your device. Deleting the App removes everything it stored (steps, distance, starting point, goals and records).
