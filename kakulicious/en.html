Kakulicious Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 | 日本語

"Kakulicious" (the "App") is an iOS application provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy describes how user information is handled in the App.

1. Our Principle — Video and Audio Never Leave Your Device

  • Camera & Microphone: The App shows the camera feed with a "choppy" look, and records video with audio only while you press the record button. Recordings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to the Developer. All processing happens on the device.
  • Photo Library: Used only to load a video you choose and to save the finished video. Neither leaves your device.

The Developer operates no server that collects or stores your video, audio, or personal information.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect the following information to serve ads:

InformationPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address / approximate locationRegion-appropriate ads
Device information (model, OS version)Ad display optimization and fraud prevention
  • You can decline or change tracking permission at any time in iOS (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Every feature works if you decline.
  • See Google's policies for how Google handles data.
