Brow Wow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 | 日本語 | Support

"Brow Wow" (Japanese name: まゆわん; the "App") is provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. The Developer does not receive your data

  • The Developer runs no servers. The photos or videos you choose, anything read from them, and the finished images or videos are never sent to the Developer.
  • There is no account or sign-in.

2. Photos, videos and camera

  • The App only reads the single photo or video you pick in the photo picker. It never reads your whole photo library.
  • The rear camera is used only when you choose "Take a photo", for that one shot.
  • All processing (finding the face, drawing the tape brows, exporting video) happens on your device.
  • Photo library access is "add only" and is used only to add the finished image or video when you tap "Save".
  • The microphone is not used. Videos keep the original video's sound.

3. Face data

  • What is collected: Using the device's on-device image recognition (Apple's Vision framework), the App reads only the approximate positions (image coordinates) of the eyes and nose of animals such as dogs and cats, or of human faces, in the photo or video you chose. The Android and Web versions do the same on the device (in the browser) with image-recognition models (RTMPose, YOLOX; human faces on Android use Google ML Kit).
  • How it is used: Only to decide where and at what angle to place the tape brows above the eyes. It is not used to identify any person or animal, for face recognition, to build face templates, for advertising or for analytics.
  • Storage and sharing: These coordinates are kept in memory while you edit. They are not stored, not sent off the device and not shared with any third party, including the advertising SDK.
  • Retention: They are discarded when you choose another photo or video, or close the App.

4. Finished images and videos

  • Finished images and videos are first placed in a temporary location on the device. They are added to your photo library only when you tap "Save", or handed to the destination you choose when you share.
  • Temporary files are removed by the operating system as part of the device's temporary storage.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect the following for ad delivery.

Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking)Ad personalisation and measurement

  • Your photos, videos and face data are never used for ad delivery or targeting.
  • You can refuse or change tracking at any time in iOS (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). All features work without it.
  • See Google's policies for how Google handles data.

6. Android version

  • The Android version uses Google ML Kit (face detection) on the device. ML Kit may send usage statistics (not images or face data) to Google to improve the service. See the ML Kit terms.
  • On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings.

7. Deleting data

All App data stays on your device. Deleting the App removes its settings. Images and videos you saved can be deleted in the Photos app.
