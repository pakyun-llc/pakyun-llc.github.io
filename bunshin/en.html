Bunshin Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 | 日本語 | Support

"Bunshin" (the "App") is provided by Pakyun LLC (the "Developer"). This policy explains how the App (iOS, Android and Web versions) handles information.

1. The Developer does not receive your data

  • The Developer runs no servers. Videos you record or choose, information read from them, and finished videos are never sent to the Developer.
  • There is no account or sign-in.

2. Camera, microphone and videos

  • The camera is used only to record video when you choose to record.
  • The microphone is used only to add sound to the video while recording. Audio is not analyzed, stored separately or sent anywhere.
  • When you pick a video, the App reads only the one video you selected.
  • Photo library access is used only to add the finished video when you tap Save.

3. Cutting out people

  • The App detects the outline of people in the video (which pixels are a person and which are background) on your device, to layer delayed "clones". iOS uses Apple's Vision framework; Android and Web use Google MediaPipe running on the device or in the browser.
  • The App does not identify faces, perform face recognition or create face templates. Outline data is used only in memory while processing and is not stored, not sent off the device, and not shared with third parties or ad SDKs.

4. Finished videos

  • Finished videos are kept in temporary storage on the device. They are saved to your photo library (Movies/bunshin on Android) only when you tap Save, and passed to the destination you choose when you share.
  • On the Web version, videos are processed inside the browser and downloaded to your device when you save.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads (the Web version may use Google AdSense). For ad delivery, the following may be collected:

InformationPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS only with tracking permission)Ad personalization and measurement
IP address and approximate locationRegion-appropriate ads
Device information (model, OS version, etc.)Ad optimization and fraud prevention
  • Your videos and person-outline data are never used for ad delivery or targeting.
  • You can deny or change tracking permission on iOS at any time (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in device settings. All features work either way.
  • See Google's policies for how Google handles data.

6. Android version

  • MediaPipe on Android may send usage statistics to Google to improve the feature (no video or person-outline data is included).

7. Deleting data

All App data stays on your device. Deleting the App removes its settings. Saved videos can be deleted from your Photos or Files app.
